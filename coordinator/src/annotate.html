    Quick reference

    Plot obstacle = a character wants something + something is in the way + it matters for what happens next. Scenery, a passing mention, or a general complaint is not enough. Judge only the bordered passage; the grey text around it is context. When unsure, choose “Can’t tell”. The full guide has examples.
