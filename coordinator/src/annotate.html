    Codebook, in one line each

    Plot obstacle: something in the passage that blocks, delays or endangers a character’s goal and plausibly affects what happens next. Atmosphere or a mere mention does not count.

    Judge only the bordered passage; the muted context is there to help you read it. You may choose at most two domains.

    Plot obstacle?

    yes
    An obstacle as defined above is present in the passage.
    no
    No obstacle, or only atmosphere or a passing mention.
    insufficient
    The passage and context are not enough to decide.
