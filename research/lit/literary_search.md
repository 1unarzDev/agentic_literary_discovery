## Verdict

No verified hit in this bounded sweep already measures a multi-domain census of plot obstacles, with material/social/internal locus, across approximately 15,000 English novels from 1800–1925. That precise combination remains a defensible candidate contribution, not an established worldwide priority claim. Several broader novelty claims are false or unsafe: historical computational conflict measurement already exists (Häußler & Gius); inward turn has already been tested computationally (Conroy; Radak et al.); lighting-era shifts in narrative time have already been measured at scale (Kim et al.). Make the contribution about validated **plot function**, the specific domain/locus taxonomy, joint hypotheses and confounded-null agent evaluation. None of these precedents independently identifies a causal effect of modernization.
