## Decision

The benchmark should be positioned as a **domain-grounded stress test of unsupported historical conclusions under controlled confounding**, not as a first discovery, corpus-comparison, synthetic-data, or false-positive-control benchmark. D5, LSC-Eval and POPPER deserve explicit discussion. None of the nine verified records measures historical plot obstacles across English novels, 1800–1925; that is a scoped finding about this subset, not a global novelty proof.
