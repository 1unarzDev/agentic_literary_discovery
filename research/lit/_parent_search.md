## Search protocol and limits

Search date: **2026-10-31**. This is a targeted novelty sweep, not a systematic review. Searches used web search with venue/domain restrictions, followed by official proceedings, publishers, institutional repositories, and author records. A search-engine result from an official site can verify bibliographic metadata; it does not establish that the full work was read. Some JCA, ADHO index, and OUP pages blocked direct opening. No authenticated Google Scholar or Semantic Scholar session was available; their domains were searched through web search, with sparse results. Negative search results are not evidence of exhaustive absence. Publication years below are checked against records, not search-engine “published/crawled” estimates. No paper dated after the search cutoff is used.
